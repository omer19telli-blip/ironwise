Ironwise for iPhone

What do I do today?

Ironwise is an AI gym trainer. It builds your strength plan, gives you every set and weight for today, and adapts to what you actually lift, how you slept and the equipment in your gym. The weights come from fixed coaching rules; the AI coach explains them and suggests changes you approve.

Help

How do I cancel Ironwise Pro?

Open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Ironwise Pro on your iPhone and tap Cancel Subscription, at least 24 hours before it renews. Apple handles all payments and refunds; request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I bought Pro on another iPhone

Sign in with the same Apple Account, then tap Profile → Restore purchases.

What stays free?

Your plan, Today and the live workout with automatic weights, exercise alternatives and nutrition targets. Pro adds the AI coach, meal estimates, building your plan with the coach, the readiness check-in with Apple Health, and progress charts with the weekly report.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile → Delete account. It deletes your account and all your data on our servers right away. Deleting the account doesn't cancel an App Store subscription, so cancel that in Settings too.

The sign-up email link doesn't open the app

Open the email on the iPhone where Ironwise is installed, then tap the link. It opens the app and signs you in.

Is this medical advice?

No. Ironwise gives general training and nutrition guidance. If you have a health condition, are pregnant, or feel pain, talk to a doctor before training. Stop if something hurts.
