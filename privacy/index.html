Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026

In short: we collect only what the app needs to coach you. We don't show ads, don't track you across apps or websites, and never sell your data. Your Apple Health data is used only to coach you. When you use the AI features, the data they need goes to OpenAI to write the answer; OpenAI doesn't use it to train its models.

Who we are

Ironwise is an iPhone app made by an independent developer (“we”, “us”). We decide how your data is used, as described here. Questions or requests: ahmetomertelli@icloud.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address (with Sign in with Apple, it can be Apple's private relay address) and an account ID.
  • Training: your setup answers (goal, experience, schedule, preferred split, limitations or injuries you type), your gym's equipment and machines, your plan, workouts (weights, reps, how each set felt), records, lift goals, cardio sessions and weekly reports.
  • Readiness check-ins: sleep hours, HRV, readiness score, energy and sore areas you enter or that come from Apple Health, and your choice to train lighter or as planned.
  • Apple Health (only with your permission): we read sleep, heart rate variability, resting heart rate, workouts and body weight. We never write to Apple Health.
  • Nutrition: sex, birth year, height, weight, activity level and any diet notes or health conditions you tell the coach for your targets; the meals you log (foods, portions, calories, protein, carbs, fat, fibre).
  • Coach chat: your messages to the AI coach and its answers.
  • Meal photos: sent for one estimate only (see below). We don't store them.
  • Subscription: whether Ironwise Pro is active, as reported by Apple to your iPhone. Apple handles payments; we never see your card or billing details.

We don't collect your location, contacts, browsing history or advertising identifiers, and the app contains no analytics or advertising code.

How we use it

Only to run the app for you: to build and adapt your plan, calculate today's weights and nutrition targets, show your progress and weekly report, keep your data in sync between your devices, answer you in the coach chat, estimate meals, and count your monthly AI allowance. We don't use your data for advertising, profiling or marketing, and we don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Where your data is kept

Your data is stored on your iPhone and synced to your account on Supabase, our database provider, on servers in Mumbai, India (AWS ap-south-1). Every row is tied to your account and protected so only you can read or change it. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS) and at rest.

The AI features (OpenAI)

The AI coach, meal estimates and building your plan with the coach use OpenAI's API (servers in the United States). Before you first use them, the app shows a notice and asks you to accept it. When you use them, our server sends OpenAI only what that answer needs:

  • Coach chat: your recent messages, your plan, recent workouts and lift trends, readiness check-ins (including sleep and HRV), your latest weekly report, and your nutrition data (body data, targets, today's meals).
  • Meal estimates: your meal description and/or photo. The photo is shrunk on your iPhone first and is never stored by us.
  • Building your plan: your setup answers (including limitations you typed), your gym and the exercise list.

Under OpenAI's API terms, this data is not used to train OpenAI's models. OpenAI may keep it for up to 30 days to detect abuse, then deletes it. We ask OpenAI not to store the conversations (store: false). Your OpenAI-bound requests go through our own server, so OpenAI never receives your email address. You can use the app without the AI features.

Apple Health data

Data from Apple Health is used only to coach you (readiness, cardio, body weight trends, nutrition targets). It is never used for advertising or data mining, never sold, and never shared with anyone except OpenAI as described above when you use the AI coach, which you agree to in the app. You can stop access at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Profile → Delete account deletes your account and all your data from our servers immediately. Signing out erases the copy on that iPhone. Data held by OpenAI is deleted within 30 days.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR, Türkiye's KVKK or California's CCPA), you can ask to access, correct, export or delete your data, or object to or restrict how it's used. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else, email ahmetomertelli@icloud.com. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. The legal basis for processing is our contract with you (to provide the app) and, for health data and the AI features, your explicit consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

Transfers abroad

Your data is processed in India (Supabase) and the United States (OpenAI). These providers protect transferred data with contractual safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Children

Ironwise isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
